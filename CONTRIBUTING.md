# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome for formulae, casks, templates, `lib/` helpers, and tap documentation.

1. Follow [Add or update a package](README.md#add-or-update-a-package) and [Validate a change](README.md#validate-a-change) in [README.md](README.md).
2. Treat security-sensitive edits per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
3. Repository-owned changes are under the [MIT License](LICENSE); packaged software keeps its own license.
